# What's new in Pocketful

Find music you'll love, keep it in your pocket. The bigger changes of each version, on your
computer and on your phone.

## [0.17.2] - 2026-11-04

**You'll know when there's a new version.**

- **A new version tells you**: when one is out, Pocketful says so once, as you open it, with
  Update and Not now. It looks a few times a day while it's open, and never when it's closed.
- **On your computer, Settings shows where to go**: a line at the top with Update, and a dot on
  Updates.
- **A version that has to be retired can update right there**, from its own page.
- **Whole albums no longer shows for a file with only albums**: they're whole already.
- **Everything works where the music server's search isn't offered**, whether you live there or
  your VPN comes out there: finding, playing and keeping songs, and radios, which were failing.
- **A song's radio comes even when the music server has none for it**: it then comes from the
  artists around that artist, and says so.

## [0.17.1] - 2026-11-03

**Better sound everywhere, your listening, and music on your speakers that keeps going.**
(0.16.0 and 0.17.0 never came out: everything they had is here.)

- **Better sound**: songs play and download from the best sound the music server has. On your
  computer, downloads are **MP3 at 320 kbps** by default, which plays on any computer, phone,
  car, iPod or MP3 player; or **Opus**, the same sound in less than half the space. On your
  phone, Opus by default, or MP3. Downloads you already have stay as they are.
- **Sync a folder**: keep a folder on your computer in sync with the music you choose, in MP3,
  for iTunes, a CD or another player. Pens, cards and the iPod shuffle get MP3 at 320 too.
- **Your listening**: the minutes, artists and songs you played this week, month or year, on
  your computer and your phone together, day by day, with one Share for your minutes, your
  artist or your song. Nothing leaves your devices.
- **What's new no longer skips pages** when one of its buttons takes you somewhere.
- **Share an album or an artist with its picture**, like a song.
- **Tidier downloads**: one row each, in the order you choose. Downloads that were waiting when
  Pocketful closed come back if you want them.
- **Updates install when you say**: Pocketful tells you when a new version is out.
- **On your phone, music on a speaker on your Wi-Fi keeps going** with the screen off, and the
  phone's volume buttons change the speaker's volume, in small steps.
- **A song's radio has the same Mix options** as an artist's.
- **Fixed: on your computer, Pocketful no longer grows to gigabytes of memory** during long
  downloads.
- **Covers inside your files**: a failed cover no longer leaves a whole album without one, and
  songs you already have get theirs, without downloading them again.
- **When the music server asks Pocketful to slow down**, downloads wait and carry on by
  themselves, and say when. Songs that can't come in one version (age-restricted, or that need
  an account) say so, and offer another version or your own file.
- **Top songs** start with what people play most, and the map stays on the artist you asked for.
- **Review**: the match you choose goes ahead of the queue and doesn't come back.
- **One Shuffle**, the same on a list's page and in the player.
- **Diagnostics**, off unless you turn it on: a small record that stays on your device and goes
  out only if you add it to your feedback. Off, nothing is kept.
- **Normalize volume** never changes in the middle of a song, and on your phone a search that
  gets no answer says so after 30 seconds.
- And smaller things: the map's names no longer overlap, the equalizer changes without
  restarting the song on your computer, and *Artists next door* never mixes up two artists with
  the same name.

## [0.15.0] - 2026-10-28

**Share a song with its picture, radios that sound like the artist, and a steadier Pocketful.**

- **Share a song with its picture**: a card made on your device, for a chat or a story.
- **The Pocketful mix**: an artist's radio stays with the artist and the ones around them.
- **Stream or download, when you sync** your phone and your computer.
- **Clear messages** when something goes wrong, and *Copy problem report* in *Send feedback*.
- **Steadier**: better on a VPN, with big libraries and after a restart, and many fixes.

Pocketful 0.14.0 on your computer can't install this update by itself: download this version
from the website once. Your music and settings stay.

## [0.14.0] - 2026-10-21

## [0.13.0] - 2026-10-18
