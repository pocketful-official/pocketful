# What's new in Pocketful

Find music you'll love, keep it in your pocket. Pocketful for your computer and Pocketful for
your phone grow together: each release says what changed on your computer and on your phone.

## [Unreleased]

## [0.14.0] - 2026-10-21

**Picks up where you left off.** The first release for everyone: it also brings everything
in 0.13.0, which was never published.

### On both

- **Picks up where you left off**: open Pocketful and your song and Up next are there, paused
  at the same second, with shuffle and repeat as you left them. Press play to carry on. Radios
  and long lists come back whole.
- **Changed your mind about a song that wouldn't download?** The × next to *Search again*
  takes it out of your library and your playlists. *Remove the N that failed* on a download,
  or *Remove all that failed* in Review, takes them all at once. *Undo* puts them back.
- An artist's page in your library starts with your albums and your songs. *More by* (only
  what you don't have yet) and *Fans also like* come last.
- From an album to its artist: *Go to* the artist in the album's menu, or click the artist's
  name under the album's title.
- Fixed: an artist who shares a name with another (like Drake) could open an empty page whose
  Play did nothing. Pocketful now opens the one you know, and Play always says when there's
  nothing to play.
- Fixed: *Go to album* finds older albums too.

### On your computer

- Headphones plugged in after Pocketful opened now show up in the outputs. On *Windows
  default*, the music follows when Windows switches to them, without you doing anything.

### On your phone

- **Start playing in the car** (Settings → In the car): when your phone connects to the car,
  Pocketful picks up where you left off. On by default. Your phone's media controls can also
  carry on without opening Pocketful (Android 13 and later).
- **Data used**: Settings → Downloads and storage shows how much Pocketful used this month,
  on Wi-Fi and on mobile data.
- Fixed: searching in *Artists you love* could close Pocketful.

## [0.13.0] - 2026-10-18

**Better sound, faster starts, simpler settings.**

### On both

- **Cleaner sound on loud songs**: the loudest parts of a song are no longer cut off, so
  rock, metal and loud pop sound clearer on speakers and in the car.
- **Normalize volume**, on by default in Settings → Playback and sound: every song plays at
  about the same loudness, so you don't reach for the volume between songs. Choose *Quiet*,
  *Normal* or *Loud*.
- **Starts faster**: songs start sooner, and the next one is ready before you need it.
- **Wrong version? Pick another**: from a song's menu, choose the version you want (not a
  live take or a music video). Pocketful remembers it. Downloaded songs can download the
  version you picked.
- **Keep the music going stays on track**: it follows the artists you chose, and their
  neighbours and genre, instead of drifting to other music from your history.
- **No more silence**: if a song can't play, the next one starts. If several can't, the
  player says why (no internet, the music server didn't answer) with *Try again*.
- **Simpler settings**: seven clear places: Playback and sound, Downloads and storage, Backup,
  Privacy, Updates and About (plus In the car on your phone). Dangerous actions are together
  under *Start over*, and *Reset settings* is now on your phone too.
- **Automatic backups**: once a day, to a folder you choose, keeping the last 7. You set the
  password once; the device keeps it safe.
- **More equalizer sounds**: Car, Less harsh, Warm, Less bass, Rock and Night; *Bass boost* is
  now *Bass forward*. Save your own sounds with a name; they go with your backup.
- A new welcome tour order: agree first, then a quick look at what Pocketful does, then restore
  a backup or start fresh.
- Your computer and your phone now only answer each other on your network.

### On your computer

- The Library's search is always there, at the top. *Ctrl+F* jumps to it.
- Genre names stay readable on every cover.
- *Choose file* when you bring in a library or restore a backup.
- Only one sync to a USB drive or music player at a time; asking again takes you to its progress.
- Lists open faster in big libraries.

### On your phone

- Fixed: a tap on an empty part of a page could reach the screen behind it.
- The keyboard now closes when you change page or tab, or start scrolling.
- Only one sync with your computer at a time; *Your computer* shows the one in progress.
- Clear numbers before you sync: songs to stream take no space; downloads show their size and
  your free space.
- Storage now shows inside Pocketful: what it uses and what's free.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-10-16

**Your taste from day one, your own sound, and a backup of your library.**

### On both

- **Artists you love**: a new step in the welcome tour. Pick a few artists and Discover and the
  map start from your taste. You can skip it.
- **New shelves, made from what you play**: *On Repeat*, *Forgotten Favourites* and *Waiting
  for you* in Library → For you; *Because you played…* and *Just Out* in Discover → New for
  you. Shelves that need your listening history stay hidden when it's off.
- **An equalizer** in Settings → Sound: ready-made sounds or your own five bands. It shapes
  what Pocketful plays through this device's speakers, headphones or Bluetooth.
- **Back up your library** in Settings → Backup: one file, locked with your password, with your
  songs list, playlists, history and settings (not the music itself). Restore it on either
  device, or from the welcome tour. Restoring only adds, and the equalizer stays with each
  device. A forgotten password can't be recovered.
- **What's new** now shows a few pages after an update, each with a button to try it. See them
  again in Settings → About; the full list is under *All changes*.
- The welcome tour now ends with how to connect your computer and your phone.

### On your computer

- Music files you pick join your library where they are, like folders. Your own files are
  never copied, moved or changed.
- Simpler headers: one search in Bring in, Discover's search next to its pills, no Import
  button in the Library, and Play on the left on artist pages.
- Fixed: plugging in a USB drive or a music player no longer breaks the Devices page.
- Fixed: a big import into a brand-new library could stop with an error.

### On your phone

- **Keep it playing**: on phones that stop music apps in the background (Xiaomi, Samsung,
  Huawei and others), the tour helps Pocketful keep playing.
- Fixed: after reinstalling Pocketful, playlists and downloads work again.
- A playlist you make or delete shows in your Library right away.
- Going back from pairing in the tour returns to the same page.
- *Add all* in Recently played stands out in orange.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-10-16

**Bring everything in, and always know what's happening.**

### On both

- **Your whole library, from one file**: bring in playlists and liked songs from another music
  app using a copy of your data. *How, in each app* shows the way.
- **Bring in starts with clear choices**: from this device, your whole library, or from any app.
- **Tap a download to see every song**: done, waiting, or not found and why, with *Search again*.
- **Removing music is safe**: only what Pocketful downloaded is deleted. Your own files stay.
- **Big jobs show their progress**, with Stop, while you keep listening.
- *Explore from here* always opens the map.
- Your phone and computer reconnect by themselves after Wi-Fi drops or sleep. No pairing again.
- Clearer messages when a VPN gets in the way of playback.

### On your computer

- **Search your Library** from the 🔍 (or Ctrl+K / Ctrl+F).
- Big imports show their progress from the start, with a bar and Stop.

### On your phone

- Fixed: swiping Pocketful away now stops the music on every phone.
- Music plays more reliably on a VPN and after switching networks.
- The welcome tour now covers the songs already on your phone and Pocketful in the car.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-10-15

**Look before you listen, and find every song you played.**

### On both

- **A radio opens first; only ▶ plays.** Every radio opens its own page with its songs, Play,
  Shuffle and *Add to library*. The small ▶ on *Radio from your artists* cards plays at once.
- **Mix it your way** on the radio's page: *Popular* or *Deep cuts*, where similar artists
  come from, and 50, 100 or 200 songs. What's playing doesn't change.
- **Recently played**: everything you've listened to lately, grouped by day, with **+** or
  *Add all* for songs not in your library. From *Jump back in* → *See all* or from *Up next*.
- *Jump back in* shows the songs themselves, even before your library has any.
- **Search from anywhere**: a 🔍 at the top of Discover and Bring in.
- An artist's name is never cut on *Support*, and artist pages line up their buttons.
- More room for the music: a lighter *Surprise me*, a slimmer player bar, no follower counts.
- **Your history, your rules**: songs count after 5 seconds; with the history off nothing is
  kept; deleting it empties *Recently played* too.
- The *Radio* tab is gone: radios live where you find the music.

### On your computer

- **Ctrl+K** jumps to search from anywhere.
- Your Library's numbers fit on one line.
- Short system sounds stay out of your library.

### On your phone

- Fixed: ringtones, notifications and alarms no longer slip into your library. They wait in a
  *Phone sounds* folder, off by default.
- Fixed: the keyboard in Bring in stays open as you type.
- Android Auto still starts a radio the moment you pick it.

## [0.9.2] - 2026-10-15

**Your computer and your phone now talk in private.**

### On both

- **A private line at home**: everything your computer and your phone say to each other is
  encrypted and signed, so nothing else on the network can read or fake it. Phones paired with
  an earlier version need to connect once more (Settings → Your phone → Connect).
- **Updates only from the project**: an update installs only when it carries the project's
  signature.

### On your computer

## [0.9.0] - 2026-10-14

**Go somewhere new. Bring in anything, from anywhere. And keep it all yours.**

### On both

- **Save your songs anywhere**: this device, a memory card or drive, or any folder, and bring

### On your computer

- Your Library's *For you* keeps to what's yours, with a door to Discover.

### On your phone · Android 0.9.0

- Save your songs in any folder inside Music, Download or Podcasts, on the phone or the card.
- Songs from your other apps get their real album cover too.
- Share to Pocketful from any app, right from the top of Bring in.
- Fixed: *Send feedback* fits the screen, and genre pages have more room.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-11

**Hello, Pocketful: a Discover tab, devices, CDs and a Now playing tab.**

### On your computer

- **A new name**: Pocketful. Your settings and library stay where they were.
- **Discover**: a map of similar artists (yours in orange), radios, and around 150 styles to
  explore. Try *Deep cuts* for the songs almost nobody plays.
- **Song radio**: start a mix from any song.
- **Now playing**: the song and your queue, which you can reorder, trim or clear.
- **Import a CD**: lossless, with track names, year and cover filled in.
- **Devices**: send music to USB drives, SD cards, external disks and music players, including
  the iPod shuffle (1st and 2nd generation). Pocketful only touches what it put there.
- **Edit details** of songs, albums, artists and playlists; your edits stay.
- *Edit* in your *Library*: select many things to delete, add to a playlist or edit at once.
- Covers for your playlists, and *Select* in any list to download or queue just some songs.
- Organise your music folder the way you like (by artist, year, genre…).
- **Opus**, the best quality available, with a converted copy for devices that can't play it.
- **Support the artist**: where to buy their music, back them or see them live.
- Bring in public playlists and albums from more services, or many links from a text file.
- A portable version for Windows, with a daily check for updates.
- Quick icons on every song: download, go to artist or album, song radio.
- The disclaimer, licence and third-party software open inside the app.
- A cleaner look, shorter texts and tips when you hover.
- Fixed: the play button no longer gets stuck "thinking".
