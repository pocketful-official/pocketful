Privacy. Pocketful has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. It collects no data about you, sends no telemetry and keeps nothing on servers run by the authors: not today, and not ever. Everything runs on your own device. A Spotify data export you import is read on your PC and never leaves it. To check for updates and for project notices, Pocketful reads a public file from the project's host (GitHub), which, like any website, can see your IP address. If you write to the project's address, your message is kept only as long as needed to answer it.

Other services can see your requests. To find, stream and download music, Pocketful talks directly to other services: YouTube / YouTube Music, Deezer, public Spotify pages, Spoqify, Music-Map, ListenBrainz, MusicBrainz, the Cover Art Archive, and the service of any link you paste (Apple Music, TIDAL, SoundCloud and others). Those services are not run by Pocketful. Like any website, they can see your IP address and what is asked for (for example, the playlist you bring in or the song you search for), and they may log or use it under their own privacy policies and terms. Pocketful sends them no account, name or identifier of yours. If this matters to you, use the anonymous option (Spoqify) for Spotify playlists, or a VPN.
