Contact

Something's wrong, an idea, or anything else: write to the project.

pocketful.app@proton.me

Plain text, to copy: pocketful.app@proton.me

The quickest way is Feedback in the app's Settings: it writes the email for you, in your own email app, and can add the Pocketful and Windows or Android versions (nothing else). Pocketful never sends anything by itself.

Reporting a bug

It helps a lot to have:

  • the Pocketful version (Settings → About) and whether it's Windows or Android, with its version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • whether it happens every time, and the steps to make it happen again;
  • the exact error message, or a screenshot (check it shows nothing private).

Please don't send music files, passwords, backup files or data exports.

Security problems

If you found a security problem, write here too and say so in the subject. Please give the project time to fix it before telling anyone else.

Rights holders and services

If you believe Pocketful, or anything the project publishes, infringes your rights or your terms, write here saying which material and which right are concerned. Every notice is read and answered.

Replies

Pocketful is made in spare time by a very small team, so a reply may take a while. Every message is read.

Your privacy

The project only sees what you send: the address you write from and your message. Use any address you like; it doesn't have to be one with your name. Your message is kept only as long as needed to answer it, and is never shared or used for anything else. See Privacy.
