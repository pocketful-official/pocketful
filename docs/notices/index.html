Pocketful reads and writes tags (M4A, MP3, FLAC, Ogg, WAV) with its own code, written from the public format specifications. It does not use any GPL library.

Facts and formats

  • libgpod (gtkpod project, LGPL-2.1-or-later; src/itdb_device.c Copyright (C) 2002-2007 Jorg Schuler): the iPod model-number and serial-number table Pocketful uses to recognise iPods was taken from it. libgpod and WikiPodLinux also document the iTunesSD and iTunesDB formats, which Pocketful's own code writes. No libgpod code is copied.

Online services

Pocketful reads data from these services through their public pages or APIs. Each has its own terms, which may restrict some uses; Pocketful's authors have not accepted them on anyone's behalf, and using a service through Pocketful does not change what its terms say (see DISCLAIMER.md). Pocketful is not affiliated with any of them: Spotify (public pages and embed), Deezer (public API), YouTube Music, Tidal, Apple Music and SoundCloud (public pages), Music-Map (Gnod), Spoqify, MusicBrainz, ListenBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (open data from the MetaBrainz Foundation).
