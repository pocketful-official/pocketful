Privacy

Pocketful sends nothing about you. No account, no tracking.

What Pocketful collects

Nothing. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. It sends no telemetry and keeps nothing on servers run by the project: not today, and not ever. Everything runs on your own computer and phone. A data export you bring in from another music app is read on your device and never leaves it.

When the phone app is paired with your computer, they talk directly to each other over your own Wi-Fi.

What the project's files can see

To check for updates (once a day, unless you turn it off) and for the project's notices (about once an hour), Pocketful reads public files from the project's host, GitHub. These requests carry nothing about you, but like any website, GitHub can see your IP address.

Other services

To find, play and bring in music, Pocketful talks directly to other services, such as Deezer, public Spotify pages, Spoqify, Music-Map, ListenBrainz, MusicBrainz, the Cover Art Archive, the services it finds songs on, and the service of any link you paste. They aren't run by Pocketful. Like any website, they can see your IP address and what is asked for (for example, the playlist you bring in or the song you search for), and may log it under their own privacy policies. Pocketful sends them no account, name or identifier of yours. If this matters to you, use the anonymous option for Spotify playlists (Settings → Privacy), or a VPN.

Writing to the project

If you write to the project, it sees only what you send: your address and your message. It's kept only as long as needed to answer, and never shared. Use any address you like. See Contact.

This website

No cookies, no scripts, no analytics, and nothing loaded from anywhere else. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, can see the IP addresses of visitors.
